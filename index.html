    How Tiny Terra works

    Your seed (any text, even emoji) is hashed into a number. That number drives 3D noise on a sphere, which becomes elevation, moisture and climate. Temperature falls with latitude and altitude, and each point becomes ocean, desert, forest, tundra, snow and more. No randomness, no network: the same seed and settings always build the same planet.
